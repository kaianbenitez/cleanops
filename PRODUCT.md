# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

CleanOps is used by 15 cleaning technicians through a responsive mobile web experience and by 3 office staff members through the full web application. The technicians need a fast, low-friction way to see the day, understand each job, travel, clock in and out, and record the right information. Office staff need a clear operational workspace for scheduling, customers, jobs, quoting, payroll preparation, and GHL coordination. The CEO needs configurable reports and visibility into the health of the business.

## Product Purpose

CleanOps helps a cleaning company run its daily operation in one practical system: field staff can complete jobs with less friction, office staff can navigate and coordinate work more easily, and leadership can see the business through configurable reports. Success means the team can rely on CleanOps for daily operations while adapting the system's settings, workflows, and reporting to the company's own way of working.

## Positioning

CleanOps is a configurable operations workspace for cleaning companies that makes the workday easier for cleaners, more organized for office staff, and more measurable for the owner.

## Brand Personality

Modern, efficient, practical. The product should feel calm and capable during busy workdays, with clear language and direct actions.

## Anti-references

CleanOps should not look flashy, decorative, noisy, or like a generic AI-generated SaaS dashboard. Avoid excessive gradients, ornamental animation, over-rounded cards, crowded layouts, unnecessary metrics, and interactions that make routine work take more taps.

## Design Principles

- Make the next operational action obvious.
- Prioritize speed and legibility for people using the app between jobs or on a phone.
- Let the company configure terminology, service areas, pricing, workflows, and reports to match its own process.
- Show operational truth clearly: assignments, job status, time, payroll inputs, payments, and exceptions.
- Use restraint. Every visual element and animation should improve orientation, feedback, or confidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The mobile technician experience must be comfortable to use one-handed, with large touch targets, strong text contrast, clear status language, and no reliance on color alone. The web application should remain readable at common laptop widths and support keyboard navigation for office staff. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep important content available without animation.
